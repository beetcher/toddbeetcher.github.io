Choose an opportunity worth testing.

A starting worksheet for a conversation with Black Flag. Engagement terms, scope, pricing, and responsibilities would need to be agreed.

1. Define the business outcome

What software capability or production problem matters? Who needs the result, and what would make it valuable?

2. Establish the baseline

What happens today? Include elapsed delivery time, production cost, quality, human effort, and ongoing support.

3. Name the constraints

Identify security, data, integration, regulatory, IP, and continuity requirements. Establish who has authority to make the relevant decisions.

4. Agree the evidence

What demonstration, acceptance criteria, cost measurement, and support plan would justify a next step?

5. Decide what happens next

Define a bounded evaluation and a decision point before expanding commitment.
