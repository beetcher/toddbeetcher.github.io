AI-native software manufacturing could help enterprises change production economics. BFD participates through commercial service revenue. After expenses and reserves, an agreed portion of available profit could support a proposed BFV venture-creation system. The temporary manufacturing advantage and finite initial transformation harvest provide the reason to seek enduring ownership, learning, relationships, and judgment.

The audience decision changes the opening and proof placement. The first seven panels answer customer questions; panels eight through eleven preserve the paper's strategic logic. This balance is a review decision, not a silent replacement of the paper with a sales pitch.

## Visual and interaction direction

Use a restrained navy, warm white, gold, and muted garden green palette. Serif headlines give the strategic argument an editorial character; system sans-serif body text stays readable without a font download. These are proposed choices, not an asserted Black Flag brand standard.

Borrow the references' ordinary scrolling, strong headlines, near-full-screen panels, responsive imagery, and optional depth. The `/enjoy` homepage additionally supports user-controlled image enlargement and deeper explanatory paths. Avoid importing unrelated workshop branding, registration, pricing, animated characters, and personal imagery.

Use stable panel anchors, a sequence dialog, a persistent Back/Next control, and current-panel progress. The page remains naturally scrollable, allows content taller than the viewport, and does not hijack touch scrolling. Left/right keys navigate when focus is not on an interactive element. Native dialogs support Escape dismissal. Reduced-motion preference removes smooth scrolling. All content remains available in the HTML if JavaScript fails; enhanced navigation and dialogs depend on JavaScript.

Desktop uses text-and-image compositions, alternating their order where helpful. Phone collapses to one column, retaining readable body text and all panel content. Supplied illustrations remain unchanged and available enlarged. A CSS background region is used on the opening; mobile crop refinements are parked.

## Economic and editorial guardrails

- Business Design terminology is preserved.
- BFD's capability is described in the context of an existing commercial factory; enterprise scale and actual economics are to be established with evidence.
- BFV is explicitly proposed; no signed capital arrangement, existing fund, ownership stake, return, customer win, or savings percentage is invented.
- Customer-side savings remain separate from BFD revenue. Expenses and reserves precede available profit; BFV funding has a conditional boundary.
- Both clocks remain unquantified, with ongoing service relationships allowed after the initial transformation.
- Fictional IBM claims remain inside a labeled thought experiment. Actual enterprise capability is addressed through proof questions.
- All named companies in orchard artwork are illustrative; no client, endorsement, or factual workforce classification is asserted.
- The customer's authority over staffing and restructuring is preserved, with the uncomfortable transition addressed in an expandable explanation.
