The Impact

What Changes Once It Exists

A common AI-readiness certification from NOCTI, a nonprofit, and how it grows from there

Hiring starts to flow.

People can prove they’re ready. Instead of hoping an interviewer takes their word for it, a worker can point to a certification employers already recognize. “Am I AI-ready? How would I know?” finally has an answer.

Employers can hire with confidence. They gain a common way to describe, request and verify the AI capability they want, in nearly every kind of role.

Educators get a clear target. Schools, colleges, workforce programs and employers can prepare people in their own ways, toward a destination everyone recognizes.

The certification is the unlock. When both sides trust the same signal, hiring starts to flow.

A cycle that builds on itself.

Founding sponsors fund the certification through a nonprofit sponsorship. NOCTI builds the standard with employers, educators and subject-matter experts. Education and workforce partners prepare people. NOCTI independently assesses and credentials what they can do. That capability goes to work in businesses and communities, and the measurable results show sponsors what their support made possible and inform what comes next.

AI companies help enable the ecosystem without owning the independent standard.

And this is only the foundation.

  1. Baseline certificationVerifies that people can handle AI’s rapid change. Not tied to any one tool.
  2. Occupation endorsementsAI endorsements across NOCTI’s catalog of hundreds of certifications.
  3. Refresher certificationsA way for people to show their skills are current, for schools, employers and workforce programs to use as they see fit.

It works like a commercial driver’s license: the base license comes first, and endorsements for specific kinds of work build on it. Funding the baseline opens the way to everything after it, and that benefit comes quickly.

These are certifications with deep historical precedent, now infused with the new human amplification technology of AI. And this is just the beginning.

Why speed matters.

AI is changing faster than any workforce standard ever has. Every month without a common baseline, people keep guessing whether they’re ready, and employers keep guessing whom to hire.

NOCTI is ready to build this certification now, as it has for 60 years, and make it available through workforce and education partners across the country.

What founding sponsors are part of.

Founding sponsors make the baseline possible. Through a nonprofit sponsorship, they fund NOCTI’s work to build the AI-readiness certification, the foundation everything on this page grows from.

This is how it gets started, and it needs your help.

If this is interesting to you, on your own or with a few others, the next step is a conversation, where the details can be shared.
