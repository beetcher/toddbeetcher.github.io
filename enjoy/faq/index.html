Questions?

Start here. If your question isn’t here, just ask.

Who is this for?

Busy, curious people, roughly in their 50s to 70, who’ve heard plenty about AI and aren’t sure how it fits their life. You’re still active and still up for fun. You don’t need to be technical.

I’m embarrassed that I don’t know how to use it. Is that okay?

That’s exactly who this is for. Plenty of capable, successful people feel the same way, and nobody here is behind. You’ll be in a room of people who are also figuring it out.

Do I have to be technical?

No, and I want to be clear about that. My background is very technical, but this isn’t about being technical. It’s about wanting to bring more enjoyment into your life. Learning this is no different from learning any other app. The real skill is having a conversation with an expert who’s in your pocket all day, and knowing what to expect from it.

I don’t even know what to ask. Where do I start?

That’s the most common barrier, and it’s the first thing we fix. We’ll make sure you can get it working on your phone and your computer, and I’ll show you how to ask for what you want, one simple step at a time.

What if I only want recipes and letters?

That’s wonderful. Where you take it is up to you. If all you ever use it for is a few recipes and some letters, that’s a great outcome. My job is to remove whatever has been stopping you.

Will you stay with me the whole way?

No, and that’s on purpose. I’m here to help you take the first step: get in the pool, understand how to use it on your phone and your computer, and organize some basic capabilities. After that, the journey is yours.

Where can I be trained?

Wherever you like: with friends at someone’s home, at a coffee shop, at a bar, or one-on-one in a private session. It doesn’t matter to me. See all the ways to join.

Can I do a private session?

Yes. Some people like to learn quietly and then surprise their kids or family with what they can do. More about private sessions.

Can I give this as a gift?

Yes. You can buy a session for someone you love. Here’s how.

Can I hear from people who’ve done it?

Soon. The first sessions are still ahead, and this page will fill with what real people say, in their own words.

What will we actually do?

Try it on real, everyday moments: hosting, driving, cooking, fixing something, writing to someone you love, getting out of a hassle. People share what they tried and what happened. See the curriculum for the building blocks.

Is this about getting more done?

No. It’s about enjoyment: small, memorable moments and less hassle. The idea is to use it every day, not all day, so you spend less time on a screen and get more real-life results: a dinner cooked, a note sent, a thing fixed.

Do I need to bring anything?

A phone or laptop if you have one, and your curiosity. Details will be confirmed when the date is set.

Do I need to pay for an AI account?

No. You can get started with free tools. We’ll show you what’s worth paying for, if anything, once you know what you like.

What if AI gets it wrong?

It does sometimes, and we’ll show you those misses too. Part of the fun is learning when to trust it and when to double-check. For things like electrical, gas or air conditioning, we’ll be clear about when to call a professional.

What does it cost?

Launch price: about $20 per hour for one-on-one time. Group pricing isn’t set yet; registering puts you first in line to hear it.

When is the next one, and where?

The date and place aren’t set yet. When you register, you’re telling us you’re interested, and we’ll email you the details first. Nothing to pay and nothing to commit to until then.
