Meet Doc

Doc is how I refer to communicating with AI. That way I don’t say AI, or Claude, or GPT.

Lots of people have a name for theirs. I just chose Doc. So when a panel says “See how I asked Doc,” that’s all it means: here is exactly what I typed to the AI, in my own words.

Why we care

A name turns a tool that felt like chaos into something you can simply talk to, and that’s where the “I can do that” moments start.
