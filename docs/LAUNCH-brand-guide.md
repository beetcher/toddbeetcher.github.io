# LAUNCH: brand guide for the workshop mark (draft v0, 2026-10-07)

For future developers and designers. Written from Todd's naming and logo discovery (2026-10-07) and a review of the four supplied assets. Anything marked DECIDED came from Todd. Anything marked EXPLORING or PROPOSED is not settled. Nothing here has been built on the site yet.

## 1. What LAUNCH is
- DECIDED: LAUNCH is the name of the workshop. It names what the workshop does: it escorts people from Zero State (the blank AI app screen) into AI capabilities.
- DECIDED: "I can do it / I can do that" stays the message to the visitor, the realization the workshop helps them reach. LAUNCH is the workshop's name and visual identity. They have different roles. This is not a whole-site rebrand and not a change to the website architecture.
- Scale and personality: a small Boulder AI workshop. Warm, plain-spoken, not corporate.

## 2. The wordmark
- Spelled LAUNCH, uppercase. Starting typeface: Montserrat ExtraBold, with a custom U.
- Colors: sunset oranges with subtle, flowing variations in hue (DECIDED: these variations are part of the approved look; never flatten them to one solid orange). The complete A is bright lime green. Only the LEFT upright of the U is lime green.
- The hidden discovery (DECIDED): the green A and the green left stroke of the U sit side by side and read as AI, embedded inside LAUNCH.
- The U construction (DECIDED, preserve exactly): its top aligns with the tops of the other letters; its left upright runs straight down to the shared baseline as a full-height green I; its orange rounded bowl dips below the baseline; its right upright is orange. It stays recognizable as a U.
- The meaning of the oversized U (DECIDED): "Why is the U bigger? Because you are the most important." The workshop is centered on the person gaining capabilities.

## 3. Colors (sampled from the approved transparent logo; approximate, not an official palette)
- Lime green (A and left of U): about #C9F92C
- Orange, typical: about #FC7D2D, ranging from about #ED6121 (deeper) to #FFA142 (lighter)
- Black background: pure black as in the tile and cloud assets
If a real palette is needed, sample from the original artwork rather than from these figures.

## 4. Backgrounds
- DECIDED: Todd prefers the mark on black. The orange variations and the lime AI pop much more strongly there, and it looks like a real thing.
- PROPOSED (Todd asked; not yet confirmed): treat "LAUNCH on black" as a rule wherever the mark appears. Reason: lime on white or on the site's cream (#fff8ec) loses contrast and the AI is easy to miss. The white-background version exists for documents and places where black is not possible.

## 5. Assets (all in enjoy/assets/)
- launch-tile-black.png (1312 x 1199, RGB): black rounded tile in the style of an app icon, logo on clean black, no cloud. NOTE: the corners outside the tile are white, not transparent. Make them transparent before placing the tile on a photo or colored page.
- launch-cloud.png (1698 x 926, RGBA): black cloud containing the logo plus the white text "AI capabilities workshop / Where U are the biggest part." CONCEPT image. The text is baked into the picture, so for the site build the words as live text and treat the cloud and logo as separate layers.
- launch-logo-transparent.png (1408 x 768, RGBA): the approved logo with a genuinely transparent background, made by removing the white from the approved original. Checked over black: clean edges, no fringe. This is the reference for lettering and colors.
- launch-logo-white.jpg (1408 x 768, RGB): the approved logo on white.
- Do not use any asset with a painted checkerboard or other artifacts; earlier attempts had them and are not intended assets.
- The original Gemini renderings are the source. If a vector version is ever needed, trace it from the approved render and keep the color variations.

## 6. Supporting wording
- Descriptor under the logo (working copy): "AI capabilities workshop". No "An" in front; Todd removed it on purpose because the line sits under LAUNCH.
- Tagline (working copy): "Where U are the biggest part." It connects to the larger U. Earlier wording: "Where 'you' are the most important."
- Todd prefers "capabilities" over "readiness", because the workshop helps people actually begin using AI.
- Suggestion (not decided): the U spelling belongs in the lockup, where the letter is the joke. In running text, say "you".

## 7. Treatments explored (EXPLORING, none decided)
- A cloud: a white cumulus cloud with LAUNCH inside that drifts into the hero very slowly while the page is already there, high in the upper right (about 20 to 25 percent in from the right edge, approximate). Idea: the cloud gradually darkens to black while the logo keeps its colors. Motion should be slow and subtle, a small discovery rather than an entrance.
- A black app-style tile: rounded corners, the logo on black, no cloud, maybe a gentle pulse. It could become an app identity someday; no app is planned now.
- An optional click on the tile that reveals a short message such as the oversized-U explanation. Not a navigation; simple.
- The cloud and the tile are probably alternatives rather than a pair.
- First place to consider LAUNCH: the hero of beetcher.com/enjoy. Open: whether it appears at the very top or lower in the first screen.

## 8. Practical notes for the build
- The hero already has the big pink "I Can Do That" title, the pitch paragraph over a photo, and the trial plane that flies in after about 2 seconds. LAUNCH must not compete with the title or arrive at the same time as the plane.
- Phone: there is no free upper-right corner; the mark needs its own spot. Keep the mark large enough that the green AI reads (roughly 200 px wide or more).
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Keep any animation to CSS where possible; no layout shift.
- Keep the lettering as the supplied image. Never retype it as ordinary text.

## 9. Do not
- Turn the whole U green, run green around the bottom curve of the U, or replace the logo with typed text.
- Flatten the orange gradients to a solid color.
- Rename or redesign the whole site around LAUNCH, or change the site's message.
