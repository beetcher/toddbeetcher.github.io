How it works

Every company that comes through BFN moves through the same three gates, whatever the outcome: Qualify. Validate. Graduate.

01

Idea

Founders come to BFN before anyone else has said yes — sometimes before there's a company at all, just a question worth answering.

02

Qualify

BFN doesn't start by asking what to build. It asks what actually needs to be proven — then turns that into a Validation Plan in days, not months.

03

Build

The factory manufactures evidence, not software. Founders get a working prototype built against exactly what the Validation Plan says needs testing.

04

Evidence

Real users, real usage, real signal — the smallest credible experiment that resolves the one assumption the company actually depends on.

05

Capital

Capital participates against evidence, not a pitch deck — deployed only once a Validation Plan has produced something real to evaluate.
