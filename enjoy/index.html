Imagine a small rural community in the 1800s, farming the land together.

And then one day, something changed.

Somebody handed you a tractor.

Nobody knew where it came from. Nobody knew how it worked. It just freaked out all the farmers.

And everyone was like, “What am I supposed to do with this?” The farmer was told, “Well, just figure it out.”

Whether it’s a tractor in the 1800s that magically appeared, or these AI apps that were suddenly available, it’s the same thing. Frustrating and confusing.

Don’t worry. It’s not you. It’s them. Spontaneous tractors or AI Apps raining down without any instructions.

These apps were designed by 25 year olds for 25 year olds.

No one understands how to use them spontaneously.

This is Zero State.

Zero State is that blank screen or blank stare, with a tractor, before you’ve done anything. You’re at zero too.

  1. 0Zero understanding of how to communicate with AI.
  2. 0Zero understanding of how to use AI.
  3. 0Zero understanding of what AI can do for you.

I once downloaded the wrong AI app. Sort of a scam. Then I had to figure out who even makes these.

Now forget that tractor. Remember being 15?

You couldn’t wait to be 16. Not for the car. For what the car meant.

All those exciting things you thought lay on the other side of independence and the freedom of driving.

It was never the car. It was the capability that gave you the independence, and the empowerment that came with it.

And that’s what AI can be for you. It can be a capability. As an amplifier of capability, it is totally empowering. And I’ve experienced it.

But let me tell you, after struggling through this process, I’ve come to a point where I couldn’t imagine ever going back to not having AI every day. Like driving a car. A TV. An iPhone. A flushing toilet.

And once you understand how AI can amplify your capabilities and bring real empowerment to what you’re doing for the relationships in your life, the people, places, organizations and things that matter to you.

Every day, you’ll be glad to have it.

Because you empowered yourself to help the people, places, organizations and things that matter to you.
