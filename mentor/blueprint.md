## Purpose
A mobile-first, thumb-scrollable single page with two jobs:
- **Primary**: Make a 15-22 year old say *"I want to show up"*
- **Secondary**: Give Jeremiah (and partners like him) something to validate — *"Would your students resonate with this?"*

---

## The Name
**A Shared Moment of Growth**

---

## Core Thesis
System-level thinking is the through-line. Everything ladders up to it. Not knowing Python is a gift, not a penalty. AI removes the barrier of needing to be a coding expert — freeing young people to think at the system level. That's what nobody is teaching.

---

## Key Phrases — Never Lose These
- *"From problem to product"*
- *"A gift, not a penalty"*
- *"Project-based. Experiential. Collaborative."*
- *"Friction → idea → architecture → MVP"*
- *"Don't tend your fire alone"*
- *"A beacon, not a warning"*
- *"System-level thinking"* — the continuity word, appears in every frame

## The Frames

**Frame 1 — Opening Image (no words)**
- The wedding lift photo — Todd mid-air, arms raised, groomsmen lifting him, purple lights, pure joy
- No caption. No copy. Just the image.
- Job: stop the thumb. Make them ask *who is this guy?*

---

**Frame 2 — The Hook Questions**
Make them nod. Written for a 19-year-old on their phone.
- *Do you see problems everywhere but don't know what to do with them?*
- *Do you have ideas but no clue how to actually build something?*
- *Do you want to work with others to build something real — not just talk about it?*
- *Do you want to walk out of a room having gone from idea to prototype?*

---

**Frame 3 — The Reframe**
Counter the fear. This is the emotional core.
- This isn't a coding class
- It isn't an entrepreneurship class
- It isn't an engineering class
- It's about learning to coexist with the most powerful productivity tool ever handed to a generation
- *Not knowing Python isn't a weakness. It's a gift.*
- The cowboys who resisted trains didn't stop being cowboys. They just became irrelevant ones.

---

**Frame 4 — What This Experience Is**
- Project-based. Experiential. Collaborative.
- You'll identify real friction, architect a real solution, build a real prototype — as a team
- System-level thinking from the very first moment to the last
- No silos. No solo coding. No lectures about theory.

---

**Frame 5 — What You Walk Away With**
- Something real you can show anyone
- Proof you went from problem to product
- The confidence and identity of a system-level thinker
- *That's the new résumé*

**Frame 7 — The Manifesto**
*The cowboy image — aerial Phoenix shot, lone cowboy by fire, city built around him*

Letter to those who care. Key beats:
- The cowboy wasn't wrong. He just stopped picking up new tools.
- Being a cowboy was never about the horse. It was about contributing — to your family, your community, the people counting on you.
- That spirit is portable. Into 2025. Into AI.
- The cowboys and cowgirls who thrive won't be the ones who learned Python. They'll be the ones who learned to think at the system level.

- *Don't tend your fire alone.*
- *This is the moment. Come grow with us.*
