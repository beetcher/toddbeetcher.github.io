Imagine a small rural community in the 1800s, farming the land together.

And then one day, something changed.

Somebody handed you a tractor.

Nobody knew where it came from. Nobody knew how it worked. It just freaked out all the farmers.

And everyone was like, “What am I supposed to do with this?” The farmer was told, “Well, just figure it out.”

Whether it’s a tractor in the 1800s that magically appeared, or these AI apps that were suddenly available, it’s the same thing. Frustrating and confusing.

Don’t worry. It’s not you. It’s them. Spontaneous tractors or AI Apps raining down without any instructions.

These apps were designed by 25 year olds for 25 year olds.

No one understands how to use them spontaneously.

This is Zero State.

Zero State is that blank screen or blank stare, with a tractor, before you’ve done anything. You’re at zero too.

  2. 0Zero understanding of how to use AI.

FAQ

I’m embarrassed that I don’t know how to use it. Is that okay?

That’s exactly who this is for. Plenty of capable, successful people feel the same way, and nobody here is behind. You’ll be in a room of people who are also figuring it out.

Do I have to be technical?

No, and I want to be clear about that. My background is very technical, but this isn’t about being technical. It’s about wanting to bring more enjoyment into your life. Learning this is no different from learning any other app. The real skill is having a conversation with an expert who’s in your pocket all day, and knowing what to expect from it.

What if I only want recipes and letters?

That’s wonderful. Where you take it is up to you. If all you ever use it for is a few recipes and some letters, that’s a great outcome. My job is to remove whatever has been stopping you.

Do I need to pay for an AI account?

No. You can get started with free tools. We’ll show you what’s worth paying for, if anything, once you know what you like.

What if AI gets it wrong?

It does sometimes, and we’ll show you those misses too. Part of the fun is learning when to trust it and when to double-check. For things like electrical, gas or air conditioning, we’ll be clear about when to call a professional.

The drive home

  1. We heard rumors that parents had sued the Boulder Valley School District.

    We didn’t want to read the whole 39-page lawsuit, or all the press.

  2. So I asked Doc, my AI assistant, for a report.

    I told him how long to make it, based on how long our drive home was.

  3. We played it on the way home from breakfast.

  4. We got caught up on something we wanted to hear about, together.

    A few months ago, playing something like that in the car was awkward and weird for Suzanne. Now it’s as normal as turning on the radio.

  5. I steered Doc to make something of value for both of us.

    It was fun, too.
