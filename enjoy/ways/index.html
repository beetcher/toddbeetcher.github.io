Ways to join

Learn your way, wherever you like.

You don’t have to come to a classroom. Pick the way that feels easiest, and pick the place. A living room, a coffee shop or a bar all work for me.

With friends

Get a few friends together and learn it as a group. Learning alongside people you already like is the most fun way to do it, and people who learn together tend to stick with it. We can meet at someone’s home, at a coffee shop, at a bar, or wherever you’re comfortable.

A private session

Just you, at your pace, learning what you care about. A private session is also a great way to surprise your kids or other family with what you can suddenly do. You learn in private, then show up with new tricks. See what a private session looks like.

Give it as a gift

Know someone who’s been meaning to learn this and keeps putting it off? You can give a session to them. You buy it; they get the learning and the fun. To set one up, email Todd.

You pick the place

At home, at a friend’s, in a coffee shop, at a bar. It doesn’t matter to me. What matters is that you’re comfortable and having a good time.

Prices for groups, private sessions and gifts aren’t set yet. The FAQ has what’s decided so far. Register and you’ll hear first.
