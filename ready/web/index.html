    What READY does
    A service, not a test player.
    1. Author content
    Keep questions and answers independent. Put correctness, ambiguity and explanations on the connection inside a set.
    2. Deliver tests
    Freeze an assembly with an ID. Your caller presents it however it chooses. Archive old tests; never erase them.
    3. Record evidence
    Return selections and a score. Keep both returned and calculated scores, diagnostic detail and every linked update.
