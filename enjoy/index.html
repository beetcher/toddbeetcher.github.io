Life is full of chaos and fear and anxiety, from many different places, including the big unknown boogeyman of AI. Oddly, I arrive at daily moments of empowerment because of AI. It was a tool of chaos that became a source of capability. Empowerment is one of the most grounding emotions there is, empowerment through capability, and through the capability to help the people around you. You’d be surprised: it’s part of the antidote to the chaos, a safe haven.

“I can do that” is what you say out loud when the capability shows up. That feeling is what this workshop is named for.
