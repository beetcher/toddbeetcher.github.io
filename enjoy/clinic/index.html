The Clinic

A fun, hands-on evening for people who are curious about AI and busy living their lives.

You don’t need to be technical. You just need to be curious about what makes a day a little easier, or a lot more fun.

What it is

A relaxed, small-group clinic where we try AI together on real, everyday moments: hosting, driving, cooking, fixing something, writing to someone you love. Nobody is graded and nobody is behind.

Who it’s for

Busy people who are curious and a little unsure where to start. You might have kids who no longer need you every minute, a full calendar, and a sense that everyone else already knows something you don’t. You don’t need any of those things to join.

How it feels

Think book club, not classroom. People share what they tried, what worked, and what flopped. You’ll see the exact words that got a good result, and you’ll try a few yourself.

What you’ll take home

The habit of spotting moments. That’s the real skill: noticing, while you’re driving, cooking or writing, that this is a moment where AI could help. A few small wins, and a good story or two to tell.

Every day, not all day

The goal isn’t more screen time. It’s a little help that gives you more time away from the device and more real-life results: a dinner cooked, a note sent, a thing fixed.
