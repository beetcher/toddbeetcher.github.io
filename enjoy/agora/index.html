Welcome to the Agora.

You found the door.

In ancient Greek cities, the agora was the open square where people gathered to trade news and swap ideas. This is ours: a small place for people who’ve learned to use AI, to share the fun, surprising things they’ve done with it.

No feeds. No likes. No followers. Just good ideas, passed from hand to hand.

What’s inside

A small nugget every day. One thing Todd did with AI today, like remembering something about a neighbor so the next hello feels warm.

Example of a nugget

Ask it to keep a little note about someone you care about: the name of your neighbor’s dog, what they’re growing in the garden. Next time you see them, you remember.

Your stories. Something crazy, clever or just fun you did with AI? Share it here, and read what everyone else tried.
